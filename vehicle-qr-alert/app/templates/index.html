How it works

01

Register & add a vehicle

We generate a random, unguessable token and print it as a QR sticker. Only its hash is ever stored.

02

Anyone scans, reports

A passerby scans the sticker and describes what they see. No login required for them.

03

Risk is scored, owner notified

The message is scanned for risk keywords, scored, and the owner is notified instantly.
